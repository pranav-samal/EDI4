# 📋 Application Form Field Guide

## Complete Explanation of All Parameters

This guide explains what each field in the credit application form means and how users should calculate or rate themselves.

---

## 🆔 **BASIC PROFILE SECTION**

### 1. **Applicant Type**
**What it means:** Your primary financial activity pattern

**Options:**
- **Digital Earner (Underbanked)**: You use UPI, have bank account, work on gig platforms (Swiggy, Zomato, Uber, Ola, Urban Company, etc.)
- **Cash First (Unbanked)**: You primarily deal in cash, rely on community lending, microfinance, or remittances

**How to choose:** Select based on how you earn and manage money daily.

---

### 2. **Full Name**
**What it means:** Your legal name as per government ID

**How to fill:** Enter your complete name matching your Aadhaar/PAN card

---

### 3. **Age**
**What it means:** Your current age in years

**How to fill:** Enter a number between 18-100

---

### 4. **Phone Number**
**What it means:** Your primary contact number

**How to fill:** 10-digit mobile number where you can be reached

---

### 5. **Requested Amount (₹)**
**What it means:** How much loan money you need

**How to decide:**
- Calculate your actual need (business investment, medical bills, education fees, etc.)
- Don't request more than you can repay
- **Rule of thumb**: Your monthly EMI should not exceed 40% of your monthly income

**Example:** If you earn ₹25,000/month, your EMI should be max ₹10,000. For a 24-month loan at 12% interest, you can borrow up to ₹2,00,000.

---

### 6. **Address**
**What it means:** Your current residential address

**How to fill:** Complete address with locality, city, and PIN code

---

### 7. **Loan Purpose**
**What it means:** Why you need the money

**Options:** Business, Education, Medical, Home Improvement, Agriculture, Vehicle, Personal, Other

**How to choose:** Select the primary reason. Be honest - this helps us understand your needs better.

---

## 💰 **FINANCIAL FOOTPRINT SECTION**

### 8. **Monthly Income (₹)**
**What it means:** Your average monthly earnings from all sources

**How to calculate:**
1. Add up all income sources:
   - Salary/wages
   - Gig platform earnings (Swiggy, Zomato, Uber, etc.)
   - Business profits
   - Rental income
   - Remittances from family
   - Any other regular income

2. Calculate average over last 3 months

**Example:**
```
Month 1: ₹22,000 (gig earnings) + ₹3,000 (other) = ₹25,000
Month 2: ₹28,000 + ₹2,000 = ₹30,000
Month 3: ₹24,000 + ₹1,000 = ₹25,000
Average = (25,000 + 30,000 + 25,000) / 3 = ₹26,667
```

**Enter:** ₹26,667

---

### 9. **Monthly Expenses (₹)**
**What it means:** Your average monthly spending on necessities

**How to calculate:**
Add up these expenses:
- Rent/housing: ₹8,000
- Food/groceries: ₹5,000
- Utilities (electricity, water, gas): ₹1,500
- Phone/internet: ₹500
- Transportation: ₹2,000
- Children's education: ₹3,000
- Healthcare: ₹1,000
- Other essentials: ₹2,000

**Total:** ₹23,000

**Enter:** ₹23,000

**Note:** Don't include luxury items or entertainment - only necessities.

---

### 10. **Number of Dependents**
**What it means:** How many people rely on your income (excluding yourself)

**How to count:**
- Spouse: 1
- Children: Count each child
- Elderly parents you support: Count each
- Other family members you financially support: Count each

**Example:**
- You + Wife + 2 children + 1 elderly parent = **3 dependents** (don't count yourself)

**Enter:** 3

---

### 11. **Employment Stability (years)**
**What it means:** How long you've been in your current job/business/gig work

**How to calculate:**
- Count years and months in your current role
- Convert to decimal: 2 years 6 months = 2.5 years

**Examples:**
- Working at same company for 3 years → **3.0**
- Doing Swiggy deliveries for 1 year 3 months → **1.25**
- Running shop for 6 months → **0.5**
- Just started new job 2 months ago → **0.17**

**Why it matters:** Longer stability = more reliable income = better credit score

---

### 12. **Existing Active Loans**
**What it means:** Number of loans you're currently repaying

**How to count:**
Count ALL active loans:
- Personal loans
- Home loans
- Vehicle loans
- Education loans
- Microfinance loans
- Gold loans
- Credit card outstanding (if you carry balance)
- Informal loans from friends/family (if regular repayment)

**Example:**
- 1 bike loan + 1 personal loan + 1 microfinance loan = **3**

**Enter:** 3

**Note:** More loans = higher debt burden = lower credit score

---

### 13. **Loan Repayment History Score (0-10)** ⭐
**What it means:** How good you are at repaying loans on time

**Rating Scale:**

| Score | Meaning | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **10** | Perfect | Never missed a single payment, always paid on time or early |
| **9** | Excellent | Missed 1-2 payments in last 2 years, but paid within 15 days |
| **8** | Very Good | Missed 2-3 payments, paid within 30 days |
| **7** | Good | Occasional delays (30-60 days), but always paid eventually |
| **6** | Fair | Regular delays (60-90 days), some payments missed |
| **5** | Average | Frequent delays, missed several payments |
| **4** | Below Average | Many missed payments, some loans defaulted |
| **3** | Poor | Defaulted on 1-2 loans |
| **2** | Very Poor | Defaulted on multiple loans |
| **1** | Extremely Poor | Never repaid any loan properly |
| **0** | No History | Never taken a loan before |

**How to rate yourself:**

**Example 1:** You have 2 loans. In last 2 years, you paid all EMIs on time except once you were 10 days late.
→ **Score: 9**

**Example 2:** You have 1 microfinance loan. You've been 30-45 days late 3-4 times but always paid eventually.
→ **Score: 7**

**Example 3:** You took a personal loan 3 years ago and defaulted (never repaid). Now you have a new loan and paying on time.
→ **Score: 3-4**

**Example 4:** This is your first loan ever.
→ **Score: 0** (or leave blank)

### 14. **Utility Bill Payment Score (0-10)** ⭐
**What it means:** How regularly you pay electricity, water, phone, gas bills on time

**Rating Scale:**

| Score | Meaning | Description |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **10** | Perfect | Always pay before due date, never missed |
| **9** | Excellent | Paid on time 95%+ of the time |
| **8** | Very Good | Paid on time 90%+ of the time |
| **7** | Good | Paid on time 80%+ of the time, occasional 5-10 day delays |
| **6** | Fair | Paid on time 70%+ of the time, regular 10-15 day delays |
| **5** | Average | Paid on time 60%+ of the time, frequent delays |
| **4** | Below Average | Often 30+ days late |
| **3** | Poor | Frequently disconnected due to non-payment |
| **2** | Very Poor | Bills often go unpaid for months |
| **1** | Extremely Poor | Never pay on time, always in arrears |
| **0** | No History | Don't have utility bills in your name |

**How to rate yourself:**

**Example 1:** You pay electricity, water, and phone bills every month before due date using auto-pay.
→ **Score: 10**

**Example 2:** You usually pay on time, but 2-3 times a year you're 5-7 days late.
→ **Score: 8-9**

**Example 3:** You often pay 15-20 days after due date, sometimes get reminder calls.
→ **Score: 5-6**

**Example 4:** Your electricity was disconnected twice in last year due to non-payment.
→ **Score: 3**

## 📱 **FOR UNDERBANKED (Digital Earners)**

### 15. **Gig Platform Rating (0-5)** ⭐
**What it means:** Your average customer rating on gig platforms (Swiggy, Zomato, Uber, Ola, Urban Company, etc.)

**How to find:**
1. Open your gig app (Swiggy, Zomato, Uber, etc.)
2. Go to Profile or Ratings section
3. Check your overall rating

**Rating Guide:**

| Rating | Quality | Impact on Credit Score |
|--------|---------|----------------------|
| **4.5-5.0** | Excellent | Very positive |
| **4.0-4.4** | Good | Positive |
| **3.5-3.9** | Average | Neutral |
| **3.0-3.4** | Below Average | Negative |
| **Below 3.0** | Poor | Very negative |

**Example:**
- Swiggy delivery partner with 4.7 rating → **Enter: 4.7**
- Uber driver with 4.2 rating → **Enter: 4.2**
- Urban Company beautician with 3.8 rating → **Enter: 3.8**

**If you work on multiple platforms:** Calculate average
```
Swiggy: 4.6
Zomato: 4.8
Average = (4.6 + 4.8) / 2 = 4.7
```

**Enter:** 4.7

### 16. **UPI Transactions per Month**
**What it means:** How many times you use UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) in a month

**How to calculate:**
1. Open your UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, etc.)
2. Go to transaction history
3. Count transactions in last month
4. Include: Payments to merchants, bill payments, money transfers, recharges

**Example:**
```
Week 1: 8 transactions (groceries, recharge, bills)
Week 2: 6 transactions
Week 3: 9 transactions
Week 4: 7 transactions
Total = 30 transactions
```

**Enter:** 30

**Frequency Guide:**

| Transactions/Month | Activity Level | Impact |
|-------------------|----------------|--------|
| **50+** | Very High | Excellent |
| **30-49** | High | Very Good |
| **15-29** | Moderate | Good |
| **5-14** | Low | Fair |
| **0-4** | Very Low | Poor |

**Why it matters:** More UPI usage = more digital financial activity = better credit profile

## 🎯 **QUICK TIPS FOR BETTER CREDIT SCORE**

### ✅ **DO:**
1. **Be honest** - AI can detect inconsistencies
2. **Request realistic amount** - Don't over-borrow
3. **Show regular income** - Even small but consistent is good
4. **Highlight payment discipline** - High scores on repayment history
5. **Provide documents** - More proof = more trust

### ❌ **DON'T:**
1. **Exaggerate income** - Will be verified
2. **Hide existing loans** - Will be discovered
3. **Request too much** - Debt ratio matters
4. **Give fake ratings** - Can be cross-checked
5. **Leave fields blank** - Fill everything honestly

## ❓ **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS**

**Q: What if I don't have exact numbers?**
A: Give your best estimate. Be honest - approximate is better than fake.

**Q: Can I leave rating fields (0-10) blank?**
A: Yes, but filling them helps. If you don't know, rate yourself honestly based on the guide.

**Q: What if I have no loan history?**
A: Enter 0 for "Loan Repayment History Score" - it's okay to be a first-time borrower.

**Q: Should I rate myself high to get better score?**
A: No! Be honest. The AI cross-checks data. Fake ratings will hurt you more.

**Q: What if my gig rating is low (below 4.0)?**
A: Be honest. Focus on improving other factors like payment history and income stability.

**Q: How much should I request?**
A: Rule of thumb: Monthly EMI should be max 40% of your income. Use the EMI calculator on the form.
