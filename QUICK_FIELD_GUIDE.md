# 📝 Quick Field Guide - Application Form

## Rating Fields (0-10 Scale) - How to Calculate

### 🏦 **Loan Repayment History Score (0-10)**

**Quick Rating:**
- **10** = Never missed a payment, always on time
- **8-9** = Missed 1-2 payments but paid within 30 days
- **6-7** = Occasional delays (30-60 days) but always paid
- **4-5** = Frequent delays, some missed payments
- **2-3** = Defaulted on 1-2 loans
- **0-1** = Never repaid properly OR no loan history

**Example:** You have 1 loan, paid on time for 2 years except once you were 15 days late → **Score: 9**

---

### 💡 **Utility Bill Payment Score (0-10)**

**Quick Rating:**
- **10** = Always pay before due date
- **8-9** = Pay on time 90%+ of the time
- **6-7** = Usually on time, occasional 5-15 day delays
- **4-5** = Often 15-30 days late
- **2-3** = Frequently disconnected due to non-payment
- **0-1** = Never pay on time OR no bills in your name

**Example:** You pay electricity and phone bills on time every month, but twice this year you were 7 days late → **Score: 8**

---

### ⭐ **Gig Platform Rating (0-5)**
**For Underbanked Only**

**Where to find:**
- Open your gig app (Swiggy, Zomato, Uber, Ola, Urban Company)
- Go to Profile → Ratings
- Enter your overall rating

**Example:** Swiggy shows 4.7 stars → **Enter: 4.7**

**If multiple platforms:** Calculate average
```
Swiggy: 4.6 + Zomato: 4.8 = 9.4 ÷ 2 = 4.7
```

---

### 🏘️ **Community Verification Score (0-10)**
**For Unbanked Only**

**Quick Rating:**
- **9-10** = Village head/community leader vouches for you
- **7-8** = Well-known, good reputation, neighbors trust you
- **5-6** = Known in area, neutral reputation
- **3-4** = Limited community connections
- **0-2** = New to area OR bad reputation

**Example:** You've lived in the same neighborhood for 10 years, everyone knows you, no disputes → **Score: 8**

---

## Other Important Fields

### 💰 **Monthly Income**
**Add all sources:**
- Salary/wages
- Gig earnings (Swiggy, Zomato, Uber, etc.)
- Business profits
- Remittances from family
- Other regular income

**Calculate average of last 3 months**

**Example:**
```
Month 1: ₹25,000
Month 2: ₹30,000
Month 3: ₹25,000
Average = ₹26,667
```

---

### 💸 **Monthly Expenses**
**Add only necessities:**
- Rent/housing
- Food/groceries
- Utilities (electricity, water, gas)
- Phone/internet
- Transportation
- Children's education
- Healthcare
- Other essentials

**Don't include:** Entertainment, luxury items, savings

**Example:**
```
Rent: ₹8,000
Food: ₹5,000
Utilities: ₹1,500
Transport: ₹2,000
Education: ₹3,000
Other: ₹2,500
Total = ₹22,000
```

---

### 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **Number of Dependents**
**Count people who rely on your income (excluding yourself):**
- Spouse
- Children
- Elderly parents you support
- Other family members you support

**Example:** You + Wife + 2 kids + 1 parent = **3 dependents** (don't count yourself)

---

### 📅 **Employment Stability (years)**
**How long in current job/business/gig:**
- 3 years → **3.0**
- 1 year 6 months → **1.5**
- 6 months → **0.5**
- 2 months → **0.17**

**Why it matters:** Longer = more stable income = better score

---

### 🏦 **Existing Active Loans**
**Count ALL loans you're currently repaying:**
- Personal loans
- Home/vehicle loans
- Microfinance loans
- Gold loans
- Credit card outstanding
- Informal loans (if regular repayment)

**Example:** 1 bike loan + 1 personal loan = **2**

**Note:** More loans = higher debt = lower score

---

### 📱 **UPI Transactions per Month**
**For Underbanked Only**

**How to count:**
1. Open Google Pay/PhonePe/Paytm
2. Check last month's transactions
3. Count all: payments, bills, transfers, recharges

**Example:**
```
Week 1: 8 transactions
Week 2: 6 transactions
Week 3: 9 transactions
Week 4: 7 transactions
Total = 30
```

**Guide:**
- 50+ = Excellent
- 30-49 = Very Good
- 15-29 = Good
- 5-14 = Fair
- 0-4 = Poor

---

### 💰 **Savings Account Balance**
**For Underbanked Only**

**Current balance in your bank account:**
- Check banking app or passbook
- Don't include FDs or investments

**Guide:**
- ₹50,000+ = Excellent
- ₹20,000-49,999 = Good
- ₹10,000-19,999 = Moderate
- ₹5,000-9,999 = Low
- Below ₹5,000 = Very Low

---

### 📬 **Remittance Frequency per Year**
**For Unbanked Only**

**How many times you receive money from family working elsewhere:**
- Brother sends monthly = **12 times/year**
- Husband sends every 2 months = **6 times/year**
- Receive 3-4 times a year = **3-4 times/year**

---

### 🤝 **Microfinance Repayments**
**For Unbanked Only**

**Count loans you've FULLY repaid:**
- SHG (Self-Help Group) loans
- NGO loans
- Microfinance institution loans

**Example:**
- Took 2 SHG loans, repaid both = **2**
- Took 3 loans, repaid 2, still paying 1 = **2** (count only completed)
- Never taken = **0**

## 🎯 Quick Tips

- Be honest - AI detects inconsistencies
- Request realistic amount
- Fill all fields
- Provide documents

### ❌ DON'T:
- Exaggerate income
- Hide existing loans
- Give fake ratings
- Request too much

## 💡 What Affects Your Score Most

### **High Impact:**
1. Loan Repayment History (0-10)
2. Debt-to-Income Ratio
3. Requested Amount vs Income

### **Medium Impact:**
4. Utility Bill Payment (0-10)
5. Employment Stability
6. Gig Rating / Community Score

### **Lower Impact:**
7. Dependents
8. UPI Frequency
9. Savings Balance

---

## 📊 How Much to Request?

**Rule of Thumb:** Monthly EMI should be max 40% of income

**Example:**
- Income: ₹25,000/month
- Max EMI: ₹10,000/month
- For 24 months at 12% interest
- **Max loan: ₹2,00,000**

**Use the EMI calculator on the form!**

## ❓ Common Questions

**Q: What if I don't have exact numbers?**
A: Give your best honest estimate.

**Q: Can I leave rating fields blank?**
A: Yes, but filling helps your score.

**Q: What if I have no loan history?**
A: Enter 0 - it's okay to be first-time borrower.

**Q: Should I rate myself high?**
A: No! Be honest. Fake ratings hurt more.

**Q: My gig rating is low (below 4.0)?**
A: Be honest. Focus on other factors like payment history.
